# Funding Strategy

## Overview

MENDguild operates within the rapidly growing peace-tech ecosystem, where investment was projected to reach $1 billion by 2025 [3]. Our funding strategy is designed to secure the necessary capital to build, scale, and maintain our verified-anonymous cross-border dialogue platform, built on the Enclave[ZK] privacy stack by Enclave Health Technologies Inc. (EHTI) [1].

## Phase 1: Pre-Seed and Seed Stage (Grants and Accelerators)

The initial phase focuses on securing non-dilutive funding and strategic support through established peace-tech accelerators and philanthropic grants [3]. This capital will fund the MVP development, initial user acquisition, and critical security audits.

### 1. Accelerators

We will target specialized programs designed for conflict prevention startups. Key opportunities include:

*   **PeaceTech Accelerator**: A 12-week program launching in 2026 in National Landing (Arlington, VA), targeting pre-seed/seed-stage peacetech startups using software/AI for peacemaking [3]. This provides pilots with governments/enterprises and fundraising support.
*   **C5-backed PeaceTech Programs**: Ecosystem-focused efforts, including Stanford's Peace Innovation Lab [3].

### 2. Grants and Foundations

We will actively pursue funding from organizations dedicated to peacebuilding and public interest technology [3].

*   **USIP (United States Institute of Peace)**: Runs annual grant competitions for peacebuilding projects, including research, tools, and conflict prevention (awards ~$70k-$100k) [3].
*   **MacArthur Foundation**: Funds peace and security via programs like the Fund for Peace [3].
*   **Open Society Foundations**: Awards grants for transparency tech and public interest technology [3].
*   **AI for Peace (AI4P)**: DeepFunding initiative for AI innovations in peacebuilding [3].

## Phase 2: Series A and Beyond (Venture Capital and Strategic Investors)

As MENDguild demonstrates traction and the effectiveness of its AI-driven de-escalation tools, we will seek venture capital to scale operations globally [1].

### 1. Specialized Venture Capital

We will target venture capital firms specializing in peace-tech, civic tech, and dual-use technologies. Firms like Anadyr, identified as early specialized funders in the peace-tech space, are prime targets [3].

### 2. Government Programs (SBIR/STTR)

We will explore Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) programs. These fund small business R&D in tech innovation, including potential peace/conflict prevention via agencies like the DoD, NIH, and DOE (up to $1.25M phased) [3].

## Phase 3: Sustainable Revenue and Data Commons

Long-term sustainability relies on our B2B and B2G revenue models, specifically institutional licensing and early warning data subscriptions [1].

### 1. The Data Commons Model

A key component of our long-term strategy is the establishment of "Data Commons"—collaboratively governed ecosystems that pool diverse, high-quality datasets for public benefit [2]. This addresses the "data winter" in peace-tech [3]. By providing secure, ethically governed data to researchers and policymakers, we can secure ongoing funding and strategic partnerships while ensuring "do no harm" principles are upheld [2].
