## Overview

MENDguild's success depends on building a robust ecosystem of institutional partners, civil society organizations, and technology providers. Our strategy is designed to integrate our verified-anonymous cross-border dialogue platform into existing peacebuilding frameworks while addressing the critical "warning-response" gap [1]. Built on the Enclave[ZK] privacy stack by Enclave Health Technologies Inc. (EHTI), we aim to provide secure digital infrastructure at scale [1].

## Phase 1: Foundational Peacebuilding Partners

To establish credibility and drive initial adoption, we will partner with leading organizations in Track II and Track 1.5 diplomacy [3]. These organizations currently lack purpose-built, secure platforms for cross-border citizen peacebuilding [3].

### Key Targets:

*   **Search for Common Ground (SFCG)**: We will leverage their Community Dialogue Approach, a structured 4-stage process, to inform our in-room prompts and facilitation tools [3].
*   **Interpeace**: We will integrate with their participatory national dialogue methodologies, adapting their "Multifamily Healing Spaces" for digital deployment [3].
*   **United States Institute of Peace (USIP)**: We will align our platform with their "Digital Inclusion in Mediated Peace Processes" framework, utilizing their validated structural scaffolding for agenda-setting and safety protocols [3].

## Phase 2: Technology and Data Integration Partners

To enhance our AI capabilities and address the "data winter" in peace-tech, we will partner with academic institutions and technology providers [2].

### Key Targets:

*   **Early Warning Systems (VIEWS, ACLED, GDELT)**: We will integrate our aggregated, de-identified sentiment metrics with these leading systems [3]. This provides high-fidelity warnings to humanitarian organizations and diplomatic actors, addressing the "warning-response" problem [2].
*   **Academic Institutions (NYU Steinhardt, Uppsala University, PRIO)**: We will collaborate on the development of "Data Commons"—shared, ethically governed data pools for public benefit [2]. This ensures our data infrastructure serves community needs without extractive practices [2].
*   **Zero-Knowledge Technology Providers**: We ship **Semaphore**-based proofs with server-side verification today; we will evaluate **zkTLS**-style and related advances as they mature for production (research: [`docs/technical/rfc-zktls-attribute-proofs.md`](../technical/rfc-zktls-attribute-proofs.md)) [3].

## Phase 3: Grassroots and Civil Society Networks

To reach "The Concerned Citizen" and "The Diaspora Member," we must partner with local NGOs and civil society actors operating in conflict-affected regions [1].

### Key Targets:

*   **Build Up and PeaceTech Lab**: We will leverage their networks of practitioners and activists to deploy MENDguild in high-risk zones [3].
*   **Local NGOs (e.g., in the Sahel, Philippines, Kenya)**: We will provide under-resourced, locally-led NGOs with the digital infrastructure necessary to run structured sessions safely [3]. This addresses the lack of localization in existing AI models and ensures our platform supports diverse languages and cultural contexts [3].
