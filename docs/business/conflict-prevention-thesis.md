# Conflict prevention thesis (MENDguild)

**Audience:** partners, funders, internal strategy. For **diligence and engineering truth**, start with [`../../CURRENT_STATUS.md`](../../CURRENT_STATUS.md) and the [threat model](../security/threat-model.md).

## One-paragraph thesis

MENDguild aims to be **infrastructure for timely, de-escalating contact** across conflict lines: verified participation, small squads, facilitator authority, citable outcomes where appropriate, and a **disciplined path** toward early-warning style analytics (the Conflict Severity Index) under **partner governance, ethics, and law**—not a substitute for local expertise or a claim of global omniscient monitoring.

## What is strategic vs shipped

| Layer | Status |
| ----- | ------ |
| Sessions, match, verification, moderation, ledger surfaces | Shipped in product (see `CURRENT_STATUS.md`) |
| CSI rollups, regional feeds, public dashboards | **Design / pilot**; methodology in [conflict-severity-index.md](../product/conflict-severity-index.md) |
| “Lives saved” or violence probability as **fact** | **Hypothesis** until pre-registered studies and review |

## Pillars (narrative)

1. **Detection** — program-scoped listening and, over time, CSI-style composites where enabled.  
2. **Intervention** — structured squads and facilitator-led response (hours-level targets are **program design**, not a guaranteed SLA).  
3. **Measurement** — pre-registered metrics, third-party validation for strong claims.
