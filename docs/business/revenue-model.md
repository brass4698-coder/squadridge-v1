# Revenue Model

## Overview

MENDguild operates as a social enterprise, built on the Enclave[ZK] privacy stack by Enclave Health Technologies Inc. (EHTI) [1]. Our revenue model is designed to ensure financial sustainability while remaining true to our core mission: enabling verified-anonymous, cross-border dialogue for conflict prevention [1].

## B2B and B2G Monetization

Our primary revenue streams will derive from Business-to-Business (B2B) and Business-to-Government (B2G) models, targeting institutional actors engaged in Track II and Track 1.5 diplomacy [3].

### 1. Institutional Licensing (SaaS)

We will offer tiered SaaS licensing for peacebuilding organizations, NGOs, and UN agencies. These institutions currently lack purpose-built infrastructure for cross-border citizen peacebuilding and rely on repurposed, insecure tools [3].

The licensing tiers will provide access to advanced facilitator tools, organizational dashboards, and secure session management [1]. This enables "The Peace Practitioner" to run structured, secure digital dialogue sessions across conflict lines safely and at scale [1].

### 2. Early Warning Data Subscriptions

To support wider Track II diplomacy efforts, MENDguild aggregates de-identified sentiment metrics to provide early warning insights [1]. We will offer subscription access to these data streams for vetted mediators, think tanks, and policy analysts.

This service directly addresses the "warning-response" problem by providing real-time intelligence on rising regional tensions [1]. Crucially, this data is strictly separated from ephemeral messaging streams, ensuring user privacy is maintained while providing valuable intelligence [1].

## B2C Freemium Model

For "The Concerned Citizen" and "The Diaspora Member," the core platform will remain free to use [1]. This is essential for driving grassroots adoption and ensuring inclusivity, particularly in low-bandwidth environments [3].

While the core matching and dialogue features are free, we may introduce premium features for individual users, such as advanced profile verification options (e.g., zkTLS integrations for specific professional credentials) or enhanced analytics for community organizers.

## Grant and Philanthropic Funding

As a social enterprise addressing critical gaps in peace-tech infrastructure, MENDguild is well-positioned to secure significant grant and philanthropic funding [3].

Several grants, accelerators, and investors support peace-tech startups, including the USIP (United States Institute of Peace), the MacArthur Foundation, and the Open Society Foundations [3]. This funding will be crucial for initial development, deployment in high-risk zones, and the establishment of participatory data commons [2].

## Ethical Considerations

Our revenue model must never compromise our "do no harm" principles [2]. We will not monetize raw personally identifiable information (PII) or engage in extractive data practices [2]. Our focus remains on providing secure digital infrastructure that replaces conflict-amplifying social networks with trust-building dialogue platforms [1].
