# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Overview

SquadRidge enters the market to address a critical gap in the peace-tech ecosystem: the lack of a purpose-built, secure platform for cross-border citizen peacebuilding [3]. Built on the Enclave[ZK] privacy stack by Enclave Health Technologies Inc. (EHTI), our Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy focuses on establishing trust with key institutional partners while rapidly onboarding grassroots users in targeted conflict zones [1].

## Target Markets

Our initial GTM efforts will focus on regions experiencing high-intensity conflict or significant geopolitical tension, where the need for verified-anonymous dialogue is most acute. The 2026 conflict projections from the Violence and Impacts Early-Warning System (VIEWS) identify several high-risk zones, including Ukraine, Palestine/Israel, and Sudan [2].

## Market Entry Phases

### Phase 1: Institutional Partnerships (Track II Diplomacy)

The first phase focuses on establishing SquadRidge as the premier digital infrastructure for Track II and Track 1.5 diplomacy. We will partner with leading peacebuilding organizations, such as Search for Common Ground (SFCG), Interpeace, and the United States Institute of Peace (USIP) [3].

These organizations currently cobble together general-purpose tools like Zoom and Signal, which lack unified identity verification and expose participants to surveillance risks [3]. By providing a dedicated, encrypted platform with facilitator tools and organizational dashboards, SquadRidge offers a superior, secure alternative [1].

### Phase 2: Grassroots Onboarding (The Concerned Citizen)

Simultaneously, we will launch targeted campaigns to onboard "The Concerned Citizen" and "The Diaspora Member" [1]. This phase leverages the platform's zero-knowledge attribute verification, allowing users to connect safely across conflict lines without fear of state retaliation [1].

We will utilize existing networks of civil society organizations and local NGOs to drive adoption. By prioritizing structured dialogue over public broadcasting, we aim to replace conflict-amplifying social networks with our trust-building digital infrastructure [1].

### Phase 3: Data Commons and Early Warning Integration

The final phase involves integrating our aggregated, de-identified sentiment metrics with global early warning systems [1]. This addresses the "warning-response" problem by providing actionable intelligence to vetted mediators, think tanks, and UN agencies [2].

We will champion the "Data Commons" model, ensuring that our data pools are ethically governed and serve public benefit [2]. This approach positions SquadRidge not just as a dialogue platform, but as a critical component of the global peace-tech infrastructure.

## Competitive Positioning

SquadRidge uniquely combines zero-knowledge attribute verification, structured small-group matching, and AI-driven de-escalation [1]. Unlike traditional social platforms that amplify outrage, or general-purpose tools that expose identities, SquadRidge provides a secure ecosystem bridging grassroots citizen engagement with high-level institutional conflict monitoring [1].
