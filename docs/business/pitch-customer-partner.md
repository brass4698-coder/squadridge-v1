## Slide 3 — Introducing SquadRidge

**A platform designed from the ground up for high-stakes, cross-border dialogue.**

SquadRidge gives your organization a purpose-built, encrypted environment for running structured dialogue sessions — with participant anonymity protected by zero-knowledge cryptography, not just a pseudonym policy.

**What your facilitators get:**

- **Organizational dashboard** — Create and manage cohorts, sessions, and squads from a single admin view. Monitor session health in real time.
- **Structured session templates** — Pre-built dialogue frameworks (shared fears, common goals, mutual narratives) aligned with SFCG/Interpeace methodologies. Customizable for your program.
- **Facilitator intervention tools** — Real-time tone monitoring. One-click session pause. Private facilitator notes that never enter the participant view.
- **Consent and data governance controls** — Session-level consent flows, configurable data retention, and clear participant agreements — built in, not bolted on.

**What your participants get:**

- **Verified anonymity** — Their identity is never exposed. Zero-knowledge proofs confirm they meet your participation criteria (citizenship, organizational affiliation, conflict-affected status) without revealing who they are.
- **A safe room** — Time-bound sessions with AI de-escalation support. A "Slow down" button. A "Pull back" message retraction. No public broadcast, no screenshot-able public feed.
- **Structured progression** — Sessions follow dialogue frameworks that guide participants from introduction → mutual recognition → shared goals, rather than free-for-all debate.

## Slide 4 — How Zero-Knowledge Verification Works
*(No cryptography background needed)*

**The old way:** Ask participants to verify by uploading a passport, ID card, or institutional email. This works — until it doesn't. One data breach, one government subpoena, one compromised facilitator account, and you've exposed everyone in a conflict zone.

**The SquadRidge way:**

```
Participant proves:  "I am a verified citizen of Country A"
Platform confirms:  ✓  (eligible to join this squad)
Platform never sees:  Name · Location · Document · Email
```

This is powered by Semaphore-based zero-knowledge proofs — the same cryptographic family used to secure financial privacy networks. A proof is generated in the participant's browser. The server verifies the proof, not the identity.

**What this means for your program:**
- Participants in authoritarian contexts, active conflict zones, or under surveillance can join — and trust the process.
- Your organization holds no PII for bad actors to subpoena or steal.
- You can credibly assure funders and IRBs that participation is anonymous in a meaningful, technical sense.

## Slide 5 — Session Architecture

**A SquadRidge session, end to end:**

**[Onboarding]**
→ Participant lands on intent screen: *"I want to build a bridge"*
→ Non-violence norms acknowledged (structured norm-setting, not just a checkbox)
→ ZK attribute verification (citizenship or org role confirmed; no PII stored)
→ Matching queue: participant waits while algorithm builds a balanced squad

**[Squad Formation]**
→ 4–6 participants matched across conflict lines
→ Session opens with facilitator-defined prompts
→ Real-time Supabase Realtime messaging (end-to-end structured, not open chat)

**[Live Session]**
→ AI tone monitor runs continuously (local in-browser worker — no data leaves the device)
→ Slow down button available to any participant
→ Facilitator can view session health dashboard, insert prompts, or pause
→ Pull back: any message can be retracted within a grace window

**[Close & Retention]**
→ Ephemeral message data deleted per configurable retention policy
→ De-identified sentiment aggregates preserved (with participant consent)
→ Facilitator session notes exportable
→ Post-session survey prompt (optional, org-configurable)

## Slide 9 — The Bigger Picture

**Your sessions generate data that could save lives — if it's handled right.**

Every dialogue session on SquadRidge generates sentiment signals: rising tension, softening tone, recurring fear narratives, emerging common ground. Today, that intelligence evaporates when the session ends.

With your permission and under strict ethical governance, SquadRidge aggregates de-identified sentiment metrics that can flow into early warning systems like VIEWS and ACLED. This turns your dialogue program from a closed loop into a contribution to the global conflict prevention architecture.

You retain full control over your organization's data. Participation in the Data Commons is opt-in, governed participatorily, and never involves raw session content or PII.
