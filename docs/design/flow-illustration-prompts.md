# SquadRidge Flow illustration prompts

> **Optional asset prompts** — not design-system source of truth. Prefer live tokens and components in [`squadridge-trust-ui-kit.md`](./squadridge-trust-ui-kit.md).

Pasteable prompts for Google Flow (or similar) image generation. Keep **product forms** as abstract instrument UI; use these scenes for **marketing** surfaces only.

Security pages stay schematic — see [`institutional-visual-system.md`](institutional-visual-system.md). Do not use lifestyle or padlock-on-shield art on `/security`.

## Prompt formula

```
[Subject] + [Action] + [Location/context] + [Composition] + [Lighting/mood] + [Style/medium]
```

In Flow image mode: set aspect ratio (e.g. 16:9 hero, 1:1 icons) and model, then paste a prompt below.

## Brand clause (prepend)

Use this short clause so visuals stay consistent:

> In the visual style of a modern, trustworthy conflict-resolution SaaS platform called SquadRidge, with clean UI-inspired geometry, soft rounded shapes, and a calm color palette of deep navy, teal, and warm neutrals.

Then add the specific scene.

## Abstract vs human-centric

| Surface | Direction |
| ------- | --------- |
| Form pages / product controls | Abstract product-UI (geometry, glass, ledger structure) — no characters over fields |
| Landing / how-it-works / use-case | Human-centric narrative scenes (prompts below) |
| `/security` | Schematic / document-instrument only |

## Hero illustration (16:9 or 3:1)

> A wide, cinematic illustration showing a diverse group of four people seated around a circular table, collaborating calmly with a shared digital dashboard hovering in the center, representing the SquadRidge conflict-resolution ledger. The setting is a modern, minimal workspace with soft edges, abstract UI panels, and subtle data flows connecting each participant, symbolizing mediated communication and transparency. Composition is a wide shot, slight isometric angle from above, with clear negative space on the right for overlaid headline text. Lighting is soft and optimistic, with gentle gradients in deep navy, teal, and warm beige. Style is semi-flat vector with subtle depth and shadows, suitable for a web SaaS landing page.

## How it works — step visuals

### Pilot intake / request access

> An illustration of a facilitator at a laptop reviewing a structured application form on the SquadRidge platform. The screen shows abstracted form fields and a side panel labeled ‘Pilot intake’, with soft iconography for institutions and teams. The subject is calm and focused, representing manual review and co-design rather than self-serve sign-up. Medium shot from over-the-shoulder, leaving space on the left for text. Lighting is bright but gentle, color palette deep navy and teal with warm accents. Style is clean vector, minimal but expressive facial features.

### Facilitated dialogue

> Two parties in a video-style conversation layout, with a neutral facilitator in the center acting as a bridge. Abstract speech bubbles flow between them, passing through a central ‘SquadRidge’ node that symbolizes structured mediation rather than direct confrontation. Composition is a balanced triangular arrangement, with rounded UI frames and subtle timeline indicators under each participant. Lighting is warm and reassuring, with teal highlights on the facilitator. Style is semi-flat UI illustration, suitable for a product ‘how it works’ page.

### Ledger and accountability

> A stylized digital ledger panel floating over a calm abstract background, representing agreements and commitments tracked in SquadRidge. Rows show icons for agreements, timeframes, and status pills, with a subtle lock icon hinting at privacy and security. Camera is a close-up on the ledger card, slightly tilted for depth, with soft glow edges. Lighting is cool and focused, color palette navy, teal, and light gray, with one warm accent for ‘completed’ items. Style is modern UI-inspired vector illustration.

### Outcomes / peace and progress

> A group of team members standing in front of a stylized progress dashboard that shows fewer conflicts and improved trust scores over time. Lines and nodes illustrate relationships strengthening, with upward curves and checkmark icons on completed resolutions. Wide shot, but keep faces simple and friendly. Lighting is optimistic with a gentle vignette, color palette consistent with SquadRidge (navy, teal, warm neutrals). Style is clean product marketing vector art.

## Use-case — workplace teams

> A small cross-functional team in a modern office, standing around a large wall display showing the SquadRidge resolution timeline. The timeline card highlights ‘conflict detected’, ‘facilitated session’, and ‘agreement reached’ steps. Subtle icons indicate HR and leadership, but the focus is on collaborative problem-solving rather than hierarchy. Composition is a medium-wide shot with the display as the central focal point. Lighting is bright, mood constructive and professional, with navy and teal UI elements tying back to the brand. Style is semi-flat vector with rounded shapes.

## Security and privacy (marketing explainers)

Avoid generic “padlock on a shield.” Prefer mediated topology and layered visibility.

### Secure mediated space

> An abstract visualization of a secure communication space: three participants represented as simple human figures standing in separate circles, all connected via a central SquadRidge node surrounded by a translucent protective ring. Data lines carry messages through the node rather than directly between circles, symbolizing mediated, privacy-aware conflict resolution. Top-down isometric view, with subtle shield and lock motifs integrated into the ring. Lighting is cool and confident, with teal glows and deep navy background. Style is modern, minimal vector.

### Privacy-preserving ledger

> A layered card stack representing the SquadRidge ledger, where the top layer shows high-level conflict states and outcomes, while deeper layers are hinted at but visually blurred or abstracted, symbolizing privacy and limited visibility. Tiny lock icons sit on the edges of each layer. Close-up composition, slight 3D perspective, with soft gradients in navy and teal. Mood is trustworthy and technical, but still approachable. Style is clean UI card illustration.

## Icon set

Ask for “simple flat icon” and a transparent or solid background.

> Simple flat icon of two overlapping speech bubbles with a small bridge element in the middle, representing mediated conversation, in SquadRidge colors, on a transparent background, minimal, no text.

> Flat line-style icon of a conflict resolution ledger: stacked rectangles with one highlighted row and a checkmark, using navy stroke and teal accent, on solid light background.

Agent variant request:

> Generate 12 icon variations for conflict resolution, dialogue, mediation, ledger, privacy, and security in a consistent flat vector style using the SquadRidge color palette.

## Ingredients / consistency

1. Generate or upload one base SquadRidge character-set image.
2. Prompt: “Keep these characters and color palette exactly the same, but place them in a new scene that matches this description: [paste prompt].”
3. Or: “Use the attached SquadRidge hero illustration as an ingredient. Keep the color palette and character style consistent, but create a new image focused on the ledger panel in close-up, following this description: [ledger prompt].”
4. Variants: “Agent, create 6 variations of this SquadRidge hero illustration with different camera angles and lighting, keeping the brand colors and character style consistent.”

## Honest privacy note for art direction

Do not imply full platform zero-knowledge or Signal-grade E2E in illustration captions or UI copy. Prefer “mediated,” “role-scoped,” “private room,” and “approved outcome only.” See [`docs/security/threat-model.md`](../security/threat-model.md).
