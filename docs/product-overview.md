## One paragraph

SquadRidge is a structured, text-based, **facilitator-led protected dialogue platform** for mediators and peacebuilding organizations. Parties dialogue in a private messaging room; only a facilitator-approved outcome is released to a public ledger with a verification anchor — without exposing who said what. The product is in **private pilot**; it does not host video or audio calls.
