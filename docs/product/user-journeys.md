# User Journeys

## Overview

The user journeys on MENDguild are meticulously designed to bridge grassroots citizen engagement with high-level institutional conflict monitoring [1]. These distinct paths ensure that the platform's robust anonymity and low-data modes are leveraged safely, whether a user is connecting across conflict lines or analyzing aggregated early warning signals [1].

## The Concerned Citizen's Journey

The Concerned Citizen and the Diaspora Member rely heavily on the platform's zero-knowledge architecture to engage without fear of state retaliation [1].

Their journey begins with the onboarding flow, which prioritizes norm-setting by clearly explaining the platform's non-violence principles and the mechanics of verified anonymity [1]. Once onboarded, they utilize the low-data mode, which is crucial for participation in active conflict zones with severely degraded internet infrastructure [3].

Upon entering the platform, they are matched into small squads of four to six participants from opposing sides of a conflict [1]. Within these secure, time-bound dialogue sessions, in-room prompts actively guide them through structured digital dialogue frameworks, such as exploring mutual fears and shared goals, to foster sustained empathy [1]. UX copy emphasizes safety and reflection, utilizing interventions like a one-tap "Slow down" button to pause sending and a temporary "Pull back" feature for immediate message retraction [1].

## The Peace Practitioner's Journey

The Peace Practitioner utilizes facilitator tools and organizational dashboards to run structured sessions, moving beyond the limitations of general-purpose platforms like Zoom or Teams [1].

Their journey starts with the creation and management of these structured digital dialogue sessions. They leverage the platform's integration of AI-assisted real-time translation to break language barriers in multilateral workshops, enabling more inclusive participation [3].

During the sessions, they rely on AI-powered sentiment and trend analysis to gauge the public mood and understand conflict narratives in near real-time [3]. This allows them to monitor the health of the dialogue and identify when interventions are necessary, utilizing tools that analyze the dynamics of conversation to detect shifts when negotiations accelerate or stall [2].

## The Policy Analyst's Journey

The Policy Analyst monitors aggregated early warning signals to detect rising regional tensions, addressing the critical "warning-response" gap [1].

Their journey involves accessing the platform's aggregated, de-identified sentiment metrics [1]. These insights provide a high-level view of the geopolitical landscape, allowing them to track shifts in popular support or fear [3].

The data pipeline separates ephemeral, encrypted messaging streams from these aggregated analytics, ensuring that user privacy is maintained while providing valuable intelligence [1]. By utilizing these early warning systems, the analyst can provide timely insights that help human negotiators, peacekeepers, and policymakers spot rising tensions and act before violence spreads [3].
