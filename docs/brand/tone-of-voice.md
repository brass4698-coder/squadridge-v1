# Tone of Voice

## Overview

The SquadRidge tone of voice is designed to foster trust, de-escalate tension, and prioritize structured dialogue over public broadcasting [1]. Our communication must reflect the gravity of our mission—to enable verified-anonymous, cross-border dialogue—while remaining accessible and human-centric [1].

## Core Principles

### 1. Calm and Measured

Our tone is intentionally slow and deliberate, counteracting the rapid reaction times demanded by traditional social platforms [2]. We emphasize the "Power of Pause" and somatic practices as essential components of collective wellbeing [2].

*   **Do**: Use language that encourages reflection. For example, "Let's take a moment to review this message."
*   **Don't**: Use urgent or alarmist language. Avoid phrases like "Act now!" or "Immediate action required!"

### 2. Neutral and Objective

We maintain a neutral stance, avoiding inflammatory or polarized language [2]. Our role is to facilitate dialogue, not to take sides.

*   **Do**: Use factual, descriptive language. For example, "This topic has generated strong feelings."
*   **Don't**: Use emotionally charged or judgmental terms. Avoid words like "outrageous," "unacceptable," or "toxic."

### 3. Clear and Direct

Given the diverse linguistic and cultural backgrounds of our users, clarity is paramount [3]. We avoid jargon, idioms, and complex sentence structures that might be lost in translation.

*   **Do**: Use simple, straightforward sentences. For example, "Please confirm your participation."
*   **Don't**: Use overly technical or abstract language. Avoid phrases like "Leveraging our proprietary algorithms to optimize your engagement."

### 4. Empathetic and Human-Centric

While our technology is advanced, our focus remains on the human experience [2]. We acknowledge the emotional weight of conflict and the courage required to engage in cross-border dialogue.

*   **Do**: Use language that validates users' experiences. For example, "We understand this is a difficult conversation."
*   **Don't**: Be dismissive or clinical. Avoid sounding like a machine.

## In-App Interventions

Our UX copy emphasizes safety and reflection, utilizing interventions like a one-tap "Slow down" button to pause sending and a temporary "Pull back" feature for immediate message retraction [1].

*   **Slow Down Prompt**: "This message seems to be escalating. Would you like to take a moment to rephrase it?" [1]
*   **Pull Back Feature**: "You have 5 seconds to retract this message." [1]
