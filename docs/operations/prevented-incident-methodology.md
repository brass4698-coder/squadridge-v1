# Prevented incident methodology (pilot, hypothesis-grade)

**Not** a claim of realized measurement until partners pre-register definitions, baselines, and review.

## Terms

- **Incident** — defined with the partner (e.g. self-reported violence, community event counts, or external indices where legally usable).  
- **Prevented** — always a **counterfactual** claim: what would have happened without the program. In early pilots, prefer “reduced probability of self-reported harm behaviors” over headline “lives saved.”

## Scaffolding

1. **Baseline** — pre-dialogue survey: perceived likelihood of escalation or harm (Likert or bounded scale); document locale and literacy assumptions.  
2. **Post** — 7 / 30 day follow-up where consent allows; same instrument where possible.  
3. **Controls** — if randomization is infeasible, document **matched** or **historical** comparison limits in the report.

## Reporting language

- Use ranges and **confidence** language from the design (e.g. “self-reported, n=X, not independently audited”).  
- Tie any CSI mention to [conflict-severity-index.md](../product/conflict-severity-index.md) methodology and false-positive caveats.
