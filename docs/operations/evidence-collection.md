# Evidence collection (pilots)

**Purpose:** A minimal, defensible path to outcome data—aligned with [`../business/impact-metrics.md`](../business/impact-metrics.md) and the threat model. Not legal advice.

## Principles

- **Pre-register** primary metrics and limitations before the cohort starts.  
- **Separate** self-report from external validation where possible.  
- **No surprise** PII: surveys and follow-ups only within consent scope.  
- **CSI / NLP:** treat model outputs as **assistive**; human review for consequential decisions.

## Data sources (illustrative)

- Post-session and follow-up surveys (1 / 7 / 30 day if approved).  
- Facilitator rubrics and incident logs (per program).  
- Product signals only where they are **allowed** in the program (`sentiment_metrics`, aggregated CSI inputs—not raw content in external reports by default).

- Use [`../business/impact-reporting-template.md`](../business/impact-reporting-template.md) for quarterly drafts.

- In-app facilitator preflight: `/app/pilot-guide`.

## Roles

- **Product:** schema and retention alignment.  
- **Partner / PI:** survey content and local ethics.  
- **Security:** review any export of dialogue-derived features.
