# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 7, 2026 · SquadRidge is in private pilot; this policy will be reviewed by counsel before general availability._

SquadRidge is a structured, text-based protected dialogue platform for mediators and peacebuilding organizations. This policy explains what we collect, what we never collect, and how the separation between a private session room and a public released record shapes the way we handle data.

## 1. Our privacy model in one paragraph

The room and the record are architecturally separate. Session room content — facilitator prompts and participant responses, in structured text — stays in the room and is never published. Only a facilitator-approved outcome is released to the public ledger, and it never reveals who said what. Participant identity is **verified but never disclosed** — what we call directional anonymity: verified, not anonymous; private, not public.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data** for facilitators and organisations: name, work email, organisational affiliation, and role.
- **Participant verification data**: the information a facilitator requires to confirm eligibility (which may include email confirmation, identity-document review, or manual approval). This is visible only to the facilitator.
- **Session room content**: the structured text participants contribute during a session (prompts and written responses).
- **Operational and audit data**: an internal audit trail of actions (verification, room entry, prompt posted, approval given, record released), used to support the tamper-evidence of released records and to secure the platform.

## 3. What we never collect

- **No video or audio calls.** SquadRidge is a facilitator-led messaging room only. We do not capture, store, transmit, or integrate with video or audio calls.
- **No published transcript** of session dialogue. Structured text stays in the protected room until an outcome is drafted for release; only the approved outcome may become public.

## 4. What becomes public

Only the facilitator-approved outcome text and its limited metadata (organisation, date, and participant **count** — never names) are published to the public ledger, together with a verification anchor. Once released, a ledger record is permanent and tamper-evident. Public records **do not identify individual participants** and do not contain any session room content.

## 5. Identity: verified, not disclosed

Participants are verified privately by the facilitator before they enter a room. Their identity is confirmed but never appears in the released record — no names, attributions, or identifying details. Personal contact information is not shared between participants.

## 6. How we use data

We use the data above only to operate the service: to run the session lifecycle, verify participants, produce and release records, secure the platform, and communicate with you about your pilot. **We do not sell personal data, and we do not add pilot applicants to marketing lists.** Request-access details are used only to review pilot fit.

## 7. Storage, retention, and security

Session data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Room content is not retained on platform infrastructure after a session is closed and archived; an archived room is read-only and accessible only to the facilitator. We retain only the minimum needed to operate the service and to preserve the integrity of released records.

We are precise about our security boundaries: content protection today is **not** operator-proof end-to-end encryption, and we do not claim it is. Room-level end-to-end encryption is on our roadmap. See the [Security page](/security) for the full model.
