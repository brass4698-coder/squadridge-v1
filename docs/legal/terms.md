These Terms govern access to and use of SquadRidge, a structured, text-based protected dialogue platform for mediators and peacebuilding organizations. By using the platform you agree to these Terms.

## 1. What SquadRidge is — and is not

SquadRidge provides a facilitator-controlled session lifecycle (Configure → Verify → Facilitate → Release), a protected text-based dialogue room, and a public ledger of released, tamper-evident outcome records.

SquadRidge is **not** a video conferencing tool, a general-purpose messaging app, a recording platform, or a legal instrument. The platform is a facilitator-led messaging room only: it does not capture, store, transmit, or integrate with video or audio, and it does not generate transcripts of spoken sessions.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

- **Facilitators** control the session lifecycle. The facilitator is responsible for the content of any released outcome, for configuring verification appropriately, and for the decision to release. SquadRidge does not author outcomes and does not release anything on its own.
- **Participants** agree to complete the verification the facilitator requires, to engage in good faith and within the facilitator's ground rules, and **not to record, reproduce, or share session room content** outside the session without explicit facilitator approval.
- **SquadRidge** is a process platform and **is not a party** to the mediated dialogue or to any agreement reached. We do not verify the substance of released records — only that the release process was followed.

## 3. The room and the record

Session room content is private and access-controlled: access is role-based and is revoked automatically when a session closes. Room content is never published and can never become part of the public record except through content the facilitator explicitly includes in an approved outcome.

Released records are public, permanent, and tamper-evident. Each carries a verification anchor that anyone can check independently. Once released, a record cannot be altered; a facilitator or organisation administrator may withdraw a record, in which case the ledger shows a withdrawal notice at that record's ID.

## 4. Verification and access
