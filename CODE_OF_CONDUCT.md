# Code of Conduct

**SquadRidge is a space for people navigating real conflict — that raises the bar for how we treat each other.**

---

## Our pledge

We commit to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, level of experience, or beliefs.

---

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Disagree constructively. Debate ideas, not people.
- Listen. Especially when someone is sharing something hard.
- Respect confidentiality — what's said in a squad stays there.
- Acknowledge mistakes and make repairs.

---

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Deliberately undermining someone's anonymity.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith contributions.
- Any conduct that would be harmful to someone in a vulnerable situation.

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation, email the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the contact). Reports are handled confidentially. We take all reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, suspend, or permanently remove participants who violate this code of conduct. We will explain the reason and give the person a chance to respond unless immediate action is required for safety.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channels. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.
