April 2026 · Draft · Shipped vs roadmap must stay separated

A prevention-minded thesis for investors and partners: verified cohorts, facilitator-led intervention, honest security boundaries, and a carefully scoped path toward CSI-informed operations—not a public omniscient feed in the current release.

Problem

Escalation outruns coordination

When tensions jump across lines, the failure mode is not only “no chat”—it is missing trusted, bounded, timely structure. Public identity is unsafe; unverified rooms lack credibility for sensitive decisions.

Pillars

Detect · Intervene · Measure

Detect

At current stage: structured intake, cohort patterns, moderator observations, and a moderator-facing CSI read surface.

Intervene

Facilitator-led cohorts, moderation support, and partner-defined escalation paths—not an unconditional sub-hour response promise.

Measure

Verification completion, time to match, session completion, incidents, and facilitator feedback before stronger impact claims.

Confidential · SquadRidge

Product reality

What ships today vs roadmap

Today: verified access, small squads, sessions, moderation, ledger-style outcomes where enabled, security disclosure, and a moderator-facing CSI read route. Roadmap / pilot design: automated CSI ingestion, public or partner APIs, population-scale signal products, and guaranteed response SLAs.

Operations

Partner and pilot model

Cohort scoping, facilitator authority, success criteria, and incident owners are co-owned with the partner. We do not position as a substitute for field presence or state consent where required.

Evidence

Evaluation inputs required

External materials should stay with measurable pilot signals first: verification completion, time to match, session completion, repeat participation, incidents, and facilitator feedback. Stronger “lives saved” or early-warning utility claims need methodology and external review.

Deployment and governance

High-risk geographies require explicit risk review, data minimization, retention limits, moderator staffing, and lawful-process posture—use the operations runbook and data-retention notes as a starting point, not a blanket approval.
