FAQ

Anonymity & identity

Q: Is SquadRidge “anonymous”? A: It offers in-session pseudonymity with verification-aware accounts. The server still knows account identifiers, so this is not “no identity on the server” anonymity.

Deployment-specific verification and disclosure language should be set with partners before go-live.

FAQ

Encryption & operators

Q: Is it E2EE? A: Message bodies are encrypted before storage, but squad keys live in the current system and privileged operator paths still matter. Per-user, operator-blind E2EE is not part of the current release.

FAQ

Financial model

Q: Where do the numbers come from? A: The investor and financial decks pull from the Pitch Deck Hub scenario model: starting cash, seat-month pricing, headcount, OPEX, and fundraising ask. They are planning assumptions, not audited historicals.
