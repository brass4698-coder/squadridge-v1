Mission & thesis

Protected dialogue, verifiable outcomes

SquadRidge exists so facilitators can hold structured written dialogue where parties speak freely, knowing nothing said in the room will be published — then release only what everyone approved, with a tamper-evident anchor.

Problem

Trust structure, not message volume

Public identity

Email, docs, and open chat leak attribution or drafts before parties are ready — especially unsafe in high-stakes settings.

No credible record
