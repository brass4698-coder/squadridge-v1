Session flow

Match → room → ledger

A single narrative thread you can walk stakeholders through: intent capture, routing, in-room structure, then durable outputs.

1

Intent & match

Clarify purpose and constraints; route participants into a bounded strategy room (product surfaces vary by release).

2

Facilitator-led room

Structured dialogue norms; verified pseudonymous presence in-session per product posture — not “trust me” anonymity.

3

Ledger-grade outputs

Proposals and artifacts suitable for briefings where demo content is enabled — labeled illustrative when not operational data.

Operational risks

Known categories & mitigations

⚑

Misaligned expectations

Mitigation: written pilot charter; glossary; explicit “shipping vs roadmap” in every external readout.

◎

Data handling & retention

Mitigation: retention schedule co-signed; minimize sensitive payloads; align to your DPA posture.

◇

Insider / abuse pathways

Mitigation: facilitator moderation training; reporting path; tabletop on escalation before go-live.
